About the survey

The MNAB Public Opinion Survey collects public perspectives to help identify shared priorities and better understand the issues people care about.

Eligibility, methodology and dates

Participation is voluntary. This is a self-selected online survey and is not intended to represent a scientific sample or serve as an election forecast.

Closing date will be announced separately.

Candidate and source notes

Where candidate or public office information is included, source material will be checked and presented for survey purposes only. Choices are not endorsements.

Our promise to respondents

We want the experience to be clear, respectful, and easy to complete.

  • Your participation is voluntary.
  • Review your answers before submitting.
  • Your choices are your own and are not endorsements.
